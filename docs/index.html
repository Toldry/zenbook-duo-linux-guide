rclone for personal Google Drive

This page is the home page of a personal Google OAuth app named rclone. It isn't a product or a service: it's a Google Cloud client ID that one person created for their own use, so that the open-source program rclone, running on their own Ubuntu computer, can read and write files in their own Google Drive.

What the app does

  • Shows the owner's Google Drive as a folder on their computer (rclone mount).
  • Keeps a local folder and a Google Drive folder in sync (rclone bisync).

It runs only on the owner's computer, and it's used only with the owner's own Google account. There is no website, server or database behind it, and nobody else is meant to sign in to it.

How it's set up

The setup is described step by step in Google Drive and Proton Drive on Ubuntu, part of the Zenbook Duo Linux guide. Everyone who follows that guide creates their own client ID in their own Google Cloud project; these pages describe each of those personal apps.

Privacy

The app collects nothing and sends your data nowhere except between Google and your own computer. Details: Privacy policy.
